# RouteStress

## A route that can take a hit

Private alpha for independent international university applicants.

## The problem

An admissions plan can break when scores, deadlines, money, or available time changes.

Most shortlists show options, but do not explain what breaks or how to repair the route.

## The RouteStress loop

1. Build a short saved profile
2. Diagnose strengths, blockers, and missing facts
3. Stress test a score, timing, budget, or effort change
4. Repair the route with one clear next action

## A diagnosis people can trust

The platform shows plain language findings, ranked program recommendations, eligibility and feasibility bands, decisive requirements, and evidence for every reason.

Unknown facts remain unknown. Manual review never becomes an automatic pass.

## Privacy and transparency

Applicant state stays on the device in the private alpha.

Curated program facts carry a source URL, source label, stable field identifier, and last verified date.

## Technical foundation

Saved profile and versioned catalog data feed pure deterministic domain rules.

The interface renders typed results. It never decides eligibility, ranking, breakage, or repair.

## Current status

Built: program catalog, visual shell, and guided applicant profile.

Next prerequisite: accept the constraint graph and route repair contract.

Then: diagnosis and recommendations, followed by the shock and repair lab.

## The next proof point

Show that one saved profile becomes an explainable route, then show how one changed assumption produces a repaired route.
